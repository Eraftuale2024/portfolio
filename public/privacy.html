Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website is the professional portfolio of Abraham Getachew, based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. This page explains what information the site handles.

What I collect

The only information I receive is what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address and your message. I use it to read and reply to you.

How it is sent and stored

When you submit the form, your message is sent to my email inbox through an email delivery service. I keep the message only as long as needed to reply and follow up on your enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies and does not run analytics or tracking tools at the moment. The website is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard technical server logs such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

Sharing

I do not sell your information. I share it only with the service providers needed to run this site (hosting and email delivery).

Your choices

You can ask me to delete your messages or correct your details at any time by emailing abrahamgetachew2024@gmail.com.

Changes

If I add features such as analytics or a message database, I will update this page.
